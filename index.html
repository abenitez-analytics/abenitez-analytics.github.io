About me

Building the pipeline, not just the dashboard

I'm a data professional based in Barcelona working across analytics and data engineering — I build the pipelines that collect and structure data, the warehouses that model it, and the dashboards that turn it into decisions.

I believe data only creates value when the full chain behind it is solid — from ingestion through to the insight someone actually acts on. That's why my projects span Python data pipelines and Microsoft Fabric Lakehouse/Warehouse architecture as much as they do Power BI and DAX. With over seven years of experience across banking, customer service, and supply chain, I bring a strong grasp of business operations to technical work that's built to scale, not just to demo well. Currently completing an MSc in Business Analytics & Artificial Intelligence while preparing for the Microsoft Certified: Fabric Analytics Engineer Associate certification. My goal: analytics and data engineering solutions that are technically sound end-to-end and genuinely useful to the people making decisions on top of them.

Beyond data: when I'm not building dashboards or writing SQL queries, you'll probably find me analyzing football data, training for HYROX, or exploring new technologies through personal projects — they challenge me to learn continuously and turn ideas into real-world analytics solutions.
